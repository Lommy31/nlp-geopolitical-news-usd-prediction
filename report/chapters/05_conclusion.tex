\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
On 235 held-out decisions, no text representation or fusion component improved next-fixing USD/IDR direction over a majority-class rule or a tuned market-only model, and no representation was distinguishable from another. The cross-attention and gate variants did not improve on mean pooling. The frozen encoders carry topical content, but we found no direction signal in it. The finding applies to this corpus, horizon, and test period, which earlier experiments had already used.

Future work should test volatility targets, since article volume tracks absolute returns ($r=0.126$, weak). It could also test whether direction is readable only on high-volatility days, a hypothesis we did not examine. It should also use timestamped, relevance-filtered articles with a longer history, to test whether direction signals are priced in before publication. Confirmatory claims need a fresh holdout.
