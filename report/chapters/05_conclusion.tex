\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
On 235 held-out decisions, no text representation or fusion component improved next-fixing USD/IDR direction over a majority-class rule or a tuned market-only model. Paired intervals included zero, and fine-tuning FinBERT gave the same result. The finding applies to this corpus, horizon, and sample size, and the test period was not untouched. Future work should add timestamped articles, a labeled geopolitical-relevance filter, and a longer history, and should compare targets only on data not used for selection.
